# Foundations of the Agentic Enterprise — Open Course Materials

Free, adaptable teaching materials for a family of courses built on the book
**_What the Structure Does Not Carry_** by Georg Zangl (2026), foreword by
Egbert U. Imomoh.

These are complete, ready-to-teach packs — syllabi, slides, instructor guides,
tutorials and labs, problem sets, assessments, and projects — released so that
any instructor can pick them up, adapt them, and run the course.

> **Students do not need to buy the book.** Every pack is designed so that all
> lectures, tutorials, assignments, and exams can be completed from the provided
> materials alone. The book is recommended enrichment, never a paywall in front
> of the grade. (Instructors will get more from having a copy.)

---

## What's here

Four courses share one spine — the same law, six properties, four axioms,
evaluation protocol, and custody rules — and differ only in depth, pace, and
assessment.

| **Governing the Agentic Enterprise** | MBA / Executive | Governance, sovereignty, and cost | 🔜 Coming |

## The idea in one paragraph

An enterprise is not its systems — those get replaced — but a small set of
**properties** that must survive every boundary a piece of work crosses:
*context, authority, accountability, coordination, execution coherence,* and
*institutional memory.* For decades a hidden layer of people carried those
properties by hand across the gaps in every architecture diagram. Agentic AI
does not create a new requirement; it **withdraws that subsidy.** The courses
teach students to see the crossings, name the property at risk at each one, and
judge any architecture by a simple demand: *show the crossing, and show what
survives it.*

## How to use these materials

1. **Pick a course folder** above and open its syllabus first.
2. **Fill in the placeholders** — search each file for `[Instructor name]`,
   `[Term / year]`, `[Institution]`, and the suggested course code, and replace
   them for your institution.
3. **Export your own PDFs.** Files are provided as editable Word (`.docx`) and
   PowerPoint (`.pptx`) so you can rebrand and adjust them; export to PDF for
   students however you prefer.
4. **Adapt freely.** Swap in incidents from an industry your students know,
   re-weight the assessment, or split the slide deck by week. The spine is
   meant to be kept; everything around it is yours to change.
