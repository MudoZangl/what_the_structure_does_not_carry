# 12_Financial_Services_Certificate — PCERT-FS, a teaching programme for regulated institutions

TEACHING material, unlike folder 11. Folder 11 is guidance handed to people who already do the work; folder
12 is a six-week cohort programme that trains them. Both exist because the sector needs each, and they should
not be merged.

**PCERT-FS — Custody in the Regulated Institution: Governing Agentic AI Across the Three Lines of Defence.**
Six weeks, one 2.5-hour session per week plus an applied exercise inside the participant's own institution,
6 CEUs, capstone-assessed.

## What distinguishes it from PCERT-CA

PCERT-CA (folder 5) requires architecture or engineering-leadership experience. PCERT-FS requires the
opposite: second-line risk, model risk, compliance, internal audit, operational resilience, or finance-function
experience, and **no technical background at all**. Every exercise is executable without source code, model
weights, or training data — which is also what makes it runnable against a vendor-hosted system.

Two further differences, both deliberate:
- **Nothing new is adopted.** The course teaches that five of the six properties are already required by
  instruments the institution holds, and that its job is to find obligations that have quietly become unowned.
  Positioned as a new framework it will be rejected by second-line professionals, correctly.
- **The capstone is written in the institution's control vocabulary**, not the book's. Control owners,
  enforcement points, evidence of operating effectiveness, residual risk. A report requiring the reader to know
  what a crossing is has failed, because the reader is a risk committee that has not read the book and will not.

## The four documents

- **MZ-EDU-FS-001 — Syllabus.** House format. Six modules: the crossing as the unit; the six properties against
  the institution's own framework; determinism, enforcement and oversight that cannot scale; evidence; dependency
  and cost; protocol and capstone. Assessment 40% weekly exercises / 60% capstone.
- **MZ-EDU-FS-002 — Case pack.** Six consequence records, each traced to a court judgment, regulatory
  proceeding, official audit, or company admission: Knight Capital, Commonwealth Bank/AUSTRAC, TSB, Phoenix,
  Horizon, and risk data aggregation as the case with no incident. Only one involves anything resembling AI —
  stated openly, because it is a far stronger position than any recent AI-incident narrative. Every figure must
  be verified against the primary source before teaching.
- **MZ-EDU-FS-003 — Instructor guide.** Who is in the room by line of defence, module-by-module notes, and the
  six objections that will come with the true part of each conceded first. Includes the instruction to declare a
  supplier interest in module 1 and invite the room to run the protocol hardest against it.
- **MZ-EDU-FS-004 — Assessment kit.** Five exercise rubrics, the capstone brief, a five-criterion capstone
  rubric including "bite", a conceptual question bank with no figure-recall items, and a note on the conflict
  that arises when instructor and participants share an employer.

- **MZ-EDU-FS-005 — Cohort slides.** 35 slides, 16:9, built to the same layout grammar as the six existing
  decks: dark title and module dividers with the offset circles, eyebrow plus title on light content slides,
  two-column comparison cards, numbered-point slides, statement slides with a left rule, three-band exercise
  slides, and the shared footer. Six module dividers, five exercise slides, three consequence records, and the
  protocol table.

  Geometry was lifted from the existing decks rather than guessed: 13.333 × 7.5 in, 0.62 in left margin,
  eyebrow at 0.50 in / 10.5 pt / 8A8378 with letterspacing, title at 0.82 in / 25.5 pt, content from 1.90 in,
  footer at 7.08 in / 9 pt. Note for anyone regenerating: pptxgenjs `LAYOUT_16x9` is 10 × 5.625 in, which does
  NOT match this bundle — define a custom layout at 13.333 × 7.5 or everything below 5.6 in falls off the slide.

## Discipline

- Never teach a case as an AI failure. One of the six is, and saying so early buys credibility with an
  audience that has heard a great deal of AI-incident theatre.
- Every case ends on a question pointing at the participant's own institution. That question is the case's only
  purpose; the history is scaffolding.
- Mark on evidence recorded, never on the conclusion reached. "Not demonstrated" scores full marks when
  evidenced; an inferred pass scores nothing. State this in module 1 or it will not hold.
- Cite the respondent cut on every survey figure. Figures live in folder 8, not in these materials.
- Style matches the bundle: Stone palette, Georgia, no colour.
